## LinkedIn post

```
A first-time importer budgets $9,800 for a furniture order.

It ends up costing $11,025.

Nothing went wrong. They just don't know the formula customs uses:

1️⃣ Duty is charged on CIF: goods + insurance + freight.
   You pay duty on your shipping cost too.

2️⃣ Duty rate × CIF.
   Furniture into the UAE: 5% × $10,000 = $500.

3️⃣ VAT is charged on CIF + duty.
   Tax on tax: 5% × $10,500 = $525.

$10,000 + $500 + $525 = $11,025.

That's 12.5% above budget. On a product with a 20% margin, that's most of the profit gone.

Price on landed cost, never on purchase price.

I made a 6-minute walkthrough with the full example. Link in the first comment.
```

> Put the YouTube link in the **first comment**, not in the post body. LinkedIn tends to show posts with outbound links to fewer people.
