# 1. Niche — Shipping & import logistics for small businesses

The original prompt asks for "top 10 niches". That step is moot: the product (Trackora) already fixes the niche.
The real question is **which slice of logistics** the channel should own. Below are 10 slices, ranked.

CPM figures are **estimates** (no live data). Treat them as relative, not absolute.

| # | Sub-niche | Est. CPM | Difficulty | Trackora tool it feeds | 3-word concept | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Importing from China for small sellers | High | Medium | Customs Duty, Rates | "Import It Right" | **Core** |
| 2 | Customs duty & landed cost explained | High | Medium | Customs Duty Estimator | "Landed Cost Clarity" | **Core** |
| 3 | Container shipping for beginners (FCL/LCL, B/L, detention) | High | Medium-high | Detention Calc, container tracking | "Containers Decoded" | **Core** |
| 4 | "Where is my package" carrier fixes (DHL, FedEx, Aramex…) | Low-medium | Low | Tracking | "Package Problem Solved" | Traffic engine |
| 5 | Air freight basics (AWB, MAWB/HAWB) | High | Medium | MAWB tracking (Pro) | "Air Cargo Simplified" | Core |
| 6 | Freight forwarder business (margins, quoting) | Very high | High | Profit Calc, Document Generator | "Forwarder Profit Lab" | Later (needs credibility) |
| 7 | Export paperwork (invoice, packing list) | High | Low | Document Generator | "Paperwork Done Fast" | Good filler |
| 8 | Shipping news / port disruptions | Medium | High (constant) | Port pages | "Port Watch Weekly" | **Skip** — goes stale, needs daily work |
| 9 | Amazon FBA logistics | High | High (saturated) | Rates | — | **Skip** — crowded, off-product |
| 10 | Supply chain careers | Medium | Medium | none | — | **Skip** — no product link |

**Decision:** Slices 1, 2, 3 and 5 are the channel. Slice 4 is used sparingly for search traffic because those viewers
are consumers who rarely pay — they leave once their parcel shows up.

**Who pays Trackora:** importers, exporters, small forwarders → Pro ($4.99) and Business ($9.99). Every video should be
aimed at *them*, not at someone waiting for one parcel.

## Test before committing
Videos 1–6 of the calendar (`03-calendar.md`) cover slices 1, 2, 3 and 4. After 30 days, compare **average view duration
and clicks to track-ora.com per video**, not views. Double down on the winning slice.
