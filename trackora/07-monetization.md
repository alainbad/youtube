# 7. Monetization roadmap

**Uncomfortable maths first:** Pro is $4.99/mo ($2.49 the first month) and Business is $9.99/mo. At these prices, a video
must produce a *lot* of signups to matter. Example (all inputs are guesses; replace them with your real numbers at day 90):

| Step | Guess |
|---|---|
| Views on a search video in its first year | 5,000 |
| Click the description link | 2% → 100 visits |
| Create a free account | 15% → 15 |
| Upgrade to paid | 5% → ~1 subscriber |

So **one paid subscriber per video** is a reasonable early expectation. That's why the roadmap below doesn't rely on
Trackora subscriptions alone, and why the email list matters more than any single video.

---

## Stage 0 (now, 0 subscribers): set up the funnel

1. **UTM links on every description link** (`utm_source=youtube&utm_campaign=wkNN`), so you know which videos bring signups.
2. **Lead magnet: "The Landed Cost Checklist" (1-page PDF)**
   - Duty/VAT formula, a list of the 12 hidden import fees (detention, demurrage, storage, THC, customs broker fee…),
     and a blank worksheet.
   - Gate it behind an email signup on track-ora.com. It fits the Week 2 and Week 12 videos directly.
   - Bonus option: build the checklist with Trackora's Document Generator so the free PDF also shows off the product.
3. **Newsletter:** one email per video (template in `06-repurpose-wk02.md`).

## Stage 1 (first 1,000 subscribers): affiliates that fit

Check every program's current terms. Commissions below are **not verified**.

| Category | Examples to research | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Freight marketplaces / quoting | Freightos, Shipa Freight, other digital forwarders | High: viewers need quotes right after watching |
| Shipping-label / parcel platforms | Easyship, ShipStation, Shippo | High for e-commerce sellers |
| Cross-border payments | Wise Business, Payoneer | High: importers pay suppliers abroad |
| Sourcing | Alibaba Trade Assurance (no classic affiliate; check), sourcing agents | Medium |
| Customs brokerage software / services | Local brokers in UAE/KSA (referral deals) | Medium. Negotiate directly. |

Rule: only recommend what you would tell the viewer to use if there were no commission. One wrong recommendation costs more trust than a year of commissions earns.

## Stage 2 (~1,000 email subscribers): digital products

Pre-sell before building. Announce to the list with a payment link (Gumroad, which you already use). If fewer than ~10 people buy, refund them and don't build it.

1. **"Import Your First Container" mini-course** (est. $49–99): the whole calendar topic in order, plus templates.
2. **Freight Forwarder Starter Kit** (est. $29–49): quote templates, a margin sheet built on the Profit Calculator, and document templates. Sell it alongside a Business-plan upgrade.

## Stage 3 (~5k subscribers or ~2k average views): sponsorships

Sponsors buy **views per video**, not subscriber count. Logistics is niche, so pitch to companies in the space (freight
marketplaces, forwarders, payment platforms) rather than generic sponsors.

**Pricing:** the starting point is roughly $20–40 per 1,000 views for a B2B audience (estimate). Base it on your median
views over the last 10 videos, not your best video.

**Pitch template**

```
Subject: Sponsoring [channel] — importers & freight buyers, [median] views/video

Hi [name],

I run [channel], a YouTube channel teaching small importers and forwarders how shipping costs actually work:
duty, detention, landed cost. It's the same audience that books freight on [their product].

Last 10 videos: [median] median views, [AVD]% average view duration, audience mainly [top 3 countries].
Top video: "[title]" ([views] views).

I'd propose one 60-second integrated segment in the video on [relevant topic, e.g. "How to get freight quotes"],
where [their product] fits naturally, plus a pinned link and a newsletter mention to [N] subscribers.

Rate: $[X] for the package. Happy to share full analytics.

— Alain
```

## If time is limited

Skip Stage 2 until the email list exists. Do Stage 0 fully. Without UTM tracking and the lead magnet, you won't know
whether the channel is working, and every later stage depends on that.
