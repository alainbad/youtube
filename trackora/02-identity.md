Handle: `@trackora` (check availability first; fallback `@trackorahq`).

## Slogan
**"Shipping, explained before it costs you."**

## Audience profile
- **Who:** small importers, e-commerce sellers bringing stock from China/Turkey/EU, junior staff at freight forwarders,
  first-time exporters. Likely Middle East, Europe, US.
- **Their problem:** they pay surprise costs (duty, detention, demurrage, storage) because nobody explained the process.
- **Already tried:** asking their forwarder (slow, vague replies), Googling (forum threads, carrier PDFs).
- **What they search at 11pm:** "container stuck at customs", "how much duty will I pay", "what is demurrage",
  "tracking number not found", "MAWB vs HAWB".

## 4 content pillars

| Pillar | Type | Example | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Track It** | Search-driven | "Tracking number not found? Do these 5 checks" | Free tracking → signup |
| **Cost It** | Search + calculator demo | "How much duty will I pay importing from China?" | Customs Duty / Detention calc |
| **Paper It** | Explainer | "Bill of lading explained in 6 minutes" | Document Generator (Pro) |
| **Don't Get Burned** | Story / contrarian | "The $3,400 detention bill that was 100% avoidable" | Email alerts (Pro) |

## Unique mechanism: "The Real Number"
Every video ends on **one real number calculated live on screen** in a Trackora tool: the duty owed, the detention bill,
the landed cost. Most logistics channels explain concepts; this one shows the actual dollar figure. That doubles as the
product demo without being an ad.

## Format
Faceless is fine: screen recording + voiceover + simple motion graphics in Trackora's brand colours
(`#0a0f1e` background, `#6366f1` indigo, `#22d3ee` cyan). A face on camera raises trust for the
"Don't Get Burned" pillar; add it later if time allows.

## Competitors (verify before launch)
Freight-forwarder brand channels (Flexport, Freightos), import-coaching creators, and carrier channels (DHL, Maersk).
Difference: they either sell a service or teach broadly; this channel calculates the viewer's specific cost with a free tool.
